DompicMEDIA Custom Sound Effects Folder

Drop any of the following audio files into this folder (supported extensions: .wav, .mp3, .ogg, .m4a, .webm, .aac, .flac):

- startup     (e.g. startup.wav or startup.mp3)
- click       (e.g. click.wav or click.mp3)
- join_call   (e.g. join_call.wav or join_call.mp3)
- leave_call  (e.g. leave_call.wav or leave_call.mp3)
- mute        (e.g. mute.wav or mute.mp3)
- camera      (e.g. camera.wav or camera.mp3)
- post        (e.g. post.wav or post.mp3)
- like        (e.g. like.wav or like.mp3)

Files added here are automatically detected and played by the app when sound is enabled in Settings.
